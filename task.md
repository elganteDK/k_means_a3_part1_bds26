Part I — Discovering City Types

Use the City dataset: https://sds-aau.github.io/SDS-master/M1/data/cities.csv

• Choose a specific audience/potential customer who would be interested in understanding different
types of cities.
• Based on your audience's interests, decide whether K-means or Archetypal Analysis
(AA) is more appropriate. Explain why.
• Apply both K-means and AA and visualize the results for your chosen audience.
• Use the Adjusted Rand Index (ARI) to compare the two approaches. Do they
produce similar or different results?
• Based on your findings, decide whether to keep your original approach, switch
methods, or conclude that the difference is not substantial.
• Tell the story: What types of cities did you find, and what do they mean for your
audience?
